Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dress Up Game ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

The Game does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

The Game may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The Game may use third-party services that may collect information according to their own privacy policies. These services may include:

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Where advertising services are used, their applicable policies and settings regarding children and personalized advertising apply.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. Since most game data is stored locally on the user's device, we do not maintain a personal database of users.
